Show-level reporting has become one of the more welcome developments in programmatic CTV. Buyers who spent years doing the manual work to match impressions to Deal IDs can now see which programs their impressions ran against, a level of visibility that has vastly improved the quality of post-buy analysis and conversations with clients.
