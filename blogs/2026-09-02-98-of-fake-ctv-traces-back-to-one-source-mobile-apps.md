When CTV inventory turns out to be fake, mobile app-classified inventory is almost always where it's hiding. Peer39's benchmark data shows that approximately 98% of Fake CTV volume originates from mobile app-classified inventory, accounting for 16.03 percentage points of the 25.2% total Fake Content share in open exchange CTV. This isn't spread evenly across the bidstream.
