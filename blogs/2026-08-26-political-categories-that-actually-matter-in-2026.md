"Political content" is not one category. Treating it like one is the same mistake as keyword blocking, just one level up. A campaign attack ad, a climate policy explainer, and a hyperpartisan opinion piece all get filed under "politics," and they carry entirely different levels of risk and opportunity depending on who is buying against them.
