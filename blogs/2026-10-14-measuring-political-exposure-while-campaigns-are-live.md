With election day on November 3, the strategic decisions are made. Advertisers have chosen whether to avoid political content or activate around it, picked the categories that matter to them, and set their controls. What none of that planning can tell them is whether it is working right now, in the weeks when political spend is at its heaviest and the news cycle is moving fastest.
